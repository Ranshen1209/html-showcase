Manifesto

(02) — What the ink believes. 墨水的信条

02
Read slowly · 慢读

We grow ink that listens. Every droplet carries a billion tiny compasses, and every compass is waiting for you. Pull it close and it gathers into one dark, glossy body. Push it away and it bristles into spines. Nothing here is drawn. It is computed, sixty times a second, from the shape of your attention.

我们培养会倾听的墨水。每一滴都藏着亿万枚微小的指南针，等待你的指引。靠近，它聚成一体；推开，它竖起尖刺。这里没有任何一帧是画出来的，它由你的注意力，每秒计算六十次。
